Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use Spendly, we collect the information you provide directly, such as your name, email address, and account credentials, as well as the expense data you log — including amounts, categories, dates, and descriptions. We may also collect basic technical information, such as browser type and device information, to help keep the service running smoothly.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect to provide and improve the Spendly service, including generating your expense summaries and category breakdowns, maintaining your account, and communicating with you about service-related matters. We do not use your financial data for advertising purposes.

3. Data Storage

Your account and expense data are stored securely and retained for as long as your account remains active. We take reasonable measures to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

4. Third Party Services

Spendly does not sell or rent your personal data to third parties. We may rely on trusted third-party providers for infrastructure such as hosting, but these providers only process data on our behalf and are not permitted to use it for their own purposes.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, please reach out to us through the contact details provided on the Spendly website.
